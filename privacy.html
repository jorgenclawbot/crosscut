CROSSCUT Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

CROSSCUT Uploader is a private tool that uploads CROSSCUT's own videos to the CROSSCUT TikTok account.

What data the app uses

  • The TikTok access and refresh tokens issued when the account owner authorizes the app (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload).
  • The basic profile info of the connected account (open ID, display name) to confirm the right account is connected.

How it is stored and used

Tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer, readable only by that user, and are used only to upload videos the creator chose. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data from other TikTok users is collected.

Deleting your data

Revoke the app in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services); the locally stored tokens are then deleted. Questions: the contact below.
